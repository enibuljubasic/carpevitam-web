How we work

A different approach to counselling

Understanding matters, but on its own it rarely changes things. That is why a conversation with me is not just another analysis.

I listen to the story you tell about yourself: the words you use, the sentences you repeat, the roles you keep stepping back into. That story is not just a description of your life. It also builds it. And what has been built can be reshaped.

Three layers of work

First layer

The story you tell

We build identity through language. This is where we begin: with the words, sentences and roles we take for granted.

Second layer

Hidden patterns

Beneath the story lie deeper imprints: patterns of thought and emotion inherited from our families, beliefs we absorbed before we could choose them. We see the world through them without even knowing we carry them.

Third layer

Beneath the level of thought

When conversation reaches its limit, we go deeper, with a method that works where words cannot reach: the Mai-Tri method.

Single session: counselling or Mai-Tri

A one-to-one space to pause, look and get clear. For a specific decision, a turning point, or something that has been with you for a long time. Many people start with a single session to see how it suits them.

Counselling

In counselling, we start with what brought you here. I listen not only to what is happening, but also to how you talk about it. The key to change is often hidden in repetitions, in what goes unsaid and in the words you take for granted.

I don't give ready-made advice or recipes. Together, we look for the next step that is truly yours. After every counselling session, you receive concrete steps for working through your challenge, and follow-up support over the next two weeks.

Mai-Tri

Mai-Tri is a method of energy work, deep cleansing and alignment from Mohanji's tradition. It helps deeply rooted imprints and patterns to be released at an unconscious level, rather than simply kept under control. It doesn't require explaining, only your openness and intention.

What is a Mai-Tri session like? Mai-Tri is a gentle yet deep process, and completely safe. You lie down in a quiet space of your choice, and I work remotely, in silence, for 30 to 40 minutes. You don't need to do anything. Before the session you receive short instructions, and afterwards we talk: I share what came up, and we look together at how to carry it into everyday life.

Half of every Mai-Tri payment goes to charity: specifically, feeding hungry beings, people and animals. This keeps the exchange clean, and every session you book also feeds someone else.

Carpe Vitam: 6-week programme

For those who want to work on a pattern or issue in depth and holistically. The programme combines five counselling sessions and two Mai-Tri sessions.

  1. Week 1Counselling and first Mai-Tri session. Together we look at the pattern: where it shows up, what it costs you and what you want instead. With the first Mai-Tri session, what lies beneath it begins to loosen.
  2. Week 2Counselling. We look at what has shifted and turn it into a small daily practice.
  3. Week 3Counselling. We work with what has surfaced: memories, feelings or reactions that appear as the pattern starts to loosen.
  4. Week 4Second Mai-Tri session. We go deeper.
  5. Week 5Counselling. What has changed, what is still there, and how you carry it forward on your own.
  6. Week 6Closing session. We look back on the six weeks, name what you have gained and set up your practice for the months ahead.

Between sessions, you receive short practices for everyday life, so that change happens throughout your week, not only in our sessions.

About me

Hi,I'm Eni

Mentor and counsellor · PhD · more than 15 years of academic work with language and meaning · Mai-Tri practitioner · certified RTT hypnotherapist

My background is in language: more than fifteen years of teaching stylistics, literary analysis and discourse analysis at the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences in Split, and of studying how we build identity, and the reality we live in, through language.

I could analyse a text down to the last word and every (hidden) meaning. I did the same with the story I told about myself: I could recognise my patterns, the stories that made me smaller and what I needed to change. But I didn't know how to step into lasting change.

Then that story wrote itself into my body: I developed hypothyroidism with fairly severe symptoms. Alongside medical care, I also looked for deeper causes: working on the roots of my patterns, on emotions I had carried for years, on imprints older than me. I recovered fully and came out of that experience a different person.

After that, and similar experiences, I finally understood the difference between knowing and truly changing. I came to spirituality and personal growth as a sceptic, and I still check everything. What convinced me of what I know and work with today was not one experience, but the change in me, and then in the people I work with.

The deeper layer

Alongside conversation, I work with the Mai-Tri method, which reaches deeper than the level of thought, to patterns older than ourselves. Some call them the subconscious, some transgenerational inheritance, some karma.

My work is deeply rooted in the teachings of Mohanji and the ancient tradition of Sanatana Dharma, which has strengthened my respect for the heritage and culture of my ancestors. That is why I don't impose my spirituality: in counselling and in my work, I respect your tradition and the culture you come from.

If I assess that you need someone else (a doctor, a psychotherapist or another practitioner), I will tell you openly and refer you on. That is part of my responsibility to you.

The name

Why Carpe Vitam

We all know carpe diem, seize the day. But what truly troubles us rarely fits into a single day. Carpe vitam asks for more: a whole life, with its roots and with what is yet to blossom.

The Latin carpere also means to pick, like fruit when it is ripe: not by force and not too early. That is why change doesn't have to mean tearing down. What is good stays, what no longer serves you leaves, and from the same roots a new story grows, truer and more your own.

Carpe Vitam. Blossom in your own life.

This work may not be for you (yet) if you recognise yourself here:

  • You are not ready for change yet. And that's fine; sometimes the time needs to ripen.
  • You expect the problem to disappear through my effort alone. You are the key ingredient and the driving force of change.
  • You are looking for an instant fix after one or two sessions. Deep patterns formed over years, sometimes over generations.

What I offer is a path that is gentle, yet firm and lasting.

Questions

Frequently asked questions about counselling

What are online sessions like?

We meet on Google Meet. I send the link once your booking is confirmed. All you need is a quiet room and headphones.

Is this psychotherapy? Does it replace medical care?

No. The Mai-Tri method does not replace medical or psychological treatment. It can support a deeper understanding of yourself through inner exploration. Results depend on your engagement and your openness to letting go, so I cannot guarantee outcomes.

How do booking and cancellation work?

Payment is made in advance via PayPal, and it confirms your booking. You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 12 hours before the session. If you cancel later or do not attend, the session counts as used and the payment is not refunded. If something truly unexpected happens, we will find a solution. For the 6-week programme, the second instalment is due before the fourth session, and all sessions are used within eight weeks of the first.
